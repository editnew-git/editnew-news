If a layout will not open or shows a loading error, record the exact layout and the steps that lead to the problem. This helps support investigate the right item.

## Before you begin

Use your normal authorized account access. Keep the affected layout in place so support can investigate it.

## Gather the details

1. **Identify the layout.** Record its name and layout ID if available. Check these against the item you actually tried to open, especially if several layouts have similar names.

2. **Capture the error.** Copy the exact message or take a screenshot. Note whether it appears immediately when you open the layout or after another action.

3. **Check the scope.** If another layout is available within your normal access, try opening it. Record which layouts open and which fail. You do not need another person's login or administrator access.

4. **Describe what led to the failure.** Note when you first saw it and whether the layout was recently created or changed. Include the steps you used to reach the error.

5. **Send the findings to support.** Include the failing layout's name and ID, the error or screenshot, your reproduction steps, and the result of checking another layout. Leave the original layout available for investigation.

## Expected result

Support has enough detail to identify the failing layout and understand whether the problem affects one layout or a broader part of your account. A different layout opening successfully does not confirm that the original has been repaired.

## If you still cannot work

Tell support what you need to complete and whether another authorized layout is usable. Ask for guidance before replacing or deleting the affected layout.
