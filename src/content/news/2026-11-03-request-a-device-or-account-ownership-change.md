Ask support to coordinate a change when a S.A.M. device needs to move to another account or an account's organizational ownership needs correcting. Clear identifiers and owner authorization help avoid changes to the wrong screens, content, or network settings.

## Before you start

Involve the authorized account owner or administrator. Have the current organization, intended organization, affected screen details, and your agreed support route available.

## Prepare the request

1. **Describe the intended change.** State whether you need to move specific devices, correct an account's organization, or change who should have access. Give the current and intended organization names and explain the reason.

2. **Identify the affected equipment and content.** List the sign or player identifiers you know, physical locations, and current layout names. Include the intended layout after the change if it is already agreed. If an identifier is uncertain, ask support to confirm it before any move.

3. **Confirm authorization with support.** Have the authorized owner participate and ask what approval or ownership verification is required for this request. Do not send passwords or recovery codes as evidence.

4. **Describe the dependencies.** Tell support whether the player will stay at the same location, whether it uses wired or wireless networking, and whether its content should remain the same. Mention any planned relocation or service window. Share network credentials only through a method specifically agreed with support.

5. **Wait for confirmation of the completed change.** A submitted request is not confirmation that access or ownership has changed. Ask support to confirm the affected devices and destination account when the work is complete.

6. **Check access and playback.** Sign in using your authorized access, confirm the expected screens and layouts are available, and have an on-site contact check the intended content. Report missing access, unexpected content, or connectivity problems through the same support route.

## Expected result

Support has enough information to review the request, verify authority, and coordinate the change. After completion, you have checked the resulting access and the affected displays.

If you do not have an agreed support route, check [EditNew Support](https://support.editnew.ca). Device transfers and account hierarchy changes should remain with the responsible support or administration team.
