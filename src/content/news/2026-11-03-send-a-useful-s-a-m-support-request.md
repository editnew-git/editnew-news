If your S.A.M. screen is offline, blank, showing an error, or displaying unexpected content, a clear support request helps explain what needs attention. You do not need to know the cause before asking for help.

## Before you start

Gather the details you already have about the affected screen. If you are away from the location, ask an on-site contact for observations where possible. Only inspect equipment or take photos where it is safe to do so.

## Prepare your request

1. **Identify the screen and location.** Include the sign or player name, if you know it, and its physical location, such as “main entrance screen.” If the problem concerns content, include the layout name if known. Say which screens are affected.

2. **Describe the expected and actual result.** Explain what should appear and what you see instead. Include any visible error wording and when you first noticed the problem. Mention a relevant recent change or power interruption if you know about one. If a detail is unknown, say so.

3. **List the checks already tried.** Describe each check and its result. For example, say whether you checked the TV input or whether the player appeared online. Distinguish the TV from the separate player that runs the content. Report what you observed without assuming that an offline player is damaged.

4. **Add a readable photo or screenshot if available.** Make the affected area and any error message clear. Before sending, remove passwords, recovery codes, private remote-control links, and unrelated personal information. If you cannot read the message, say that rather than guessing its wording.

5. **Include an on-site contact where needed.** Provide a way for support to reach someone who can observe the screen. Mention access limitations, such as an inaccessible player or a missing TV remote. Share necessary contact details through your support channel.

6. **Send the request through your agreed support route.** Use the route provided for your account, or check [EditNew Support](https://support.editnew.ca) for current contact details. Review the request and attachments before sending.

## Copy this checklist

```text
Screen or player name, if known:
Layout name, if relevant:
Location and affected screen(s):
What should be showing:
What is showing now, including any error message:
When the issue was first noticed:
Relevant recent changes or interruptions:
Checks already tried and the result of each:
Photo or screenshot attached, if available:
On-site contact and any access limitations:
```

Keep passwords, recovery codes, and private remote-control links out of this checklist and its attachments.

## Expected result

Your request identifies the affected screen, describes the problem, and records the checks already completed. Support can use those details to decide what information or troubleshooting is needed next. Further questions may still be necessary.

## When you need more help

If you cannot safely access the equipment or complete a suggested check, tell support what prevents it. If the issue continues after support-guided checks, report the results and the screen's current state through the same agreed support route.
