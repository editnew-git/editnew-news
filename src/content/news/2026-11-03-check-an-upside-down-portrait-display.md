If a portrait display shows its content upside down, confirm the intended orientation and the affected sign before changing settings. Support may be able to correct the output through a setting appropriate to the player.

## Before you begin

You need a safe view of the screen and the sign or player ID if available. Any change to the player's video settings should be made by support or an authorized administrator using instructions confirmed for that hardware.

## Check the orientation

1. **Identify the affected sign.** Record its location, sign or player ID, and the layout it should display.

2. **Compare the layout with the screen.** Confirm that the intended layout is portrait. Take a photo showing the whole physical screen, including its top and bottom, so support can see the direction of the displayed content.

3. **Ask for the appropriate setting to be checked.** Send the photo and sign details to support or the authorized administrator. Ask them to verify the portrait-orientation or flip setting supported by that player.

4. **Follow the confirmed instructions.** Have the authorized person apply the appropriate setting to the identified sign. If you cannot confirm the target or the applicable control, stop and ask support.

5. **Check the whole screen afterward.** Confirm that text and images are upright and that the full layout appears in the intended portrait orientation.

## Expected result

After an appropriate correction, the physical screen shows the portrait content upright. Verify this at the screen rather than relying only on the layout Preview.

## If it is still upside down

Send support the sign and layout IDs, the player model if known, and photos before and after the change. Describe any setting that was changed.

If the installation itself needs adjustment, ask the responsible installation team to handle it.
