A weekly content plan describes what should play on each screen on particular weekdays and at particular times. Prepare the plan first, then compare it with the saved scheduling controls and the intended display.

## Before you begin

Identify the screens and layouts involved, the content for each period, and the people authorized to manage them. Make the intended local time zone explicit, especially when screens are in different locations.

This guide concerns recurring periods for individual **Media items**. It does not establish layout-level priorities or emergency-alert behavior.

## Prepare the weekly plan

1. **List the target screens.** Record which player serves each physical screen and which layout it should use.
2. **Describe each recurring period.** Write down the weekdays, start time, end time and content expected during that period. Include the intended time zone.
3. **Separate calendar limits from recurring hours.** Identify notices that should appear only between particular dates. Keep those date limits separate from the weekly plan so both can be checked.
4. **Resolve gaps and overlaps.** State what should play outside the planned periods. Flag overlapping or overnight periods and ask the person configuring them to confirm the supported behavior. Do not assume an unrestricted item becomes an exclusive fallback.
5. **Confirm time-zone handling.** Verify the target device's configured time zone and the labels used by the current scheduling form. Ask how daylight-saving changes and calendar limits affect the plan when relevant.

## Recognize the controls in the Media editor

Open the intended item's **wrench dropdown**, then select **Config/Schedule**. The matching desktop form has three separate scheduling areas:

- **Start Time/Date (UTC)** and **End Time/Date (UTC)** for the item's date/time bounds.
- **Active Days of the Month**, with numbered dates **1–31** and **Select All / Select None** controls.

The date fields and weekly grid display different time-basis labels. Do not automatically convert both groups the same way. Confirm the intended account/device configuration with your administrator or support before changing times. If the labels or screen differ, use [Check why scheduled media is missing](https://support.editnew.ca/news/2026-11-02-check-why-scheduled-media-is-missing/) to record the current form for review.

**Duration (seconds)** is a separate field for the item's playlist dwell time. It is not the length of a recurring business-hours period.

## Check the saved plan and the display

After an authorized edit, select the green **SAVE** at the top of the item form. Reopen the same item's **Config/Schedule** and compare all three scheduling areas with the written plan. A saved form confirms the stored settings; it does not prove physical playback or exact boundary timing.

Arrange an approved test on the intended screen before relying on the schedule. Observe it before, during and after a planned period, including a day when the item should run and a day when it should not. Check overnight periods, overlaps and seasonal clock changes separately when they affect your plan.

Keep the intended periods, saved settings, screens tested and actual observations together for the person maintaining the schedule.

## Expected result

You have a clear weekly plan and have compared it with the saved settings. The configured behavior has been checked on the intended physical display before it is relied on.

## When to ask for help

Contact [EditNew Support](https://support.editnew.ca) if the recurring scheduling controls are unavailable, their time basis is unclear, or overlapping rules, overnight periods or date-limited notices produce unexpected results.

Provide the target player and layout identifiers, the intended weekdays and times, the relevant time zone, the form's visible labels and what the screen actually showed.
