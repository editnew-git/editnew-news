Check the TV input and display connection separately from the player's online status.

## When this applies

Use this guide for an online S.A.M. player connected to a TV through an accessible HDMI path. Integrated LED systems, inaccessible wiring and boot failures need their own support assessment.

## Before you begin

Have the device identity and TV remote available. Record the exact TV message and current player status. If the installation is not safely accessible, stop and contact support.

## Check the display path

1. Confirm which HDMI input the player connects to.
2. Use the TV remote to select that corresponding input.
3. Check the accessible HDMI connections, including the extender when present.
4. If No Signal remains, ask support to confirm whether a known-good cable substitution is appropriate for this installation. A cable working with another source does not rule it out in this player's display path.

## Verify the result

Observe the actual panel showing the expected content. Record player status separately; an online check-in alone does not establish visible output.

## When to contact support

Stop if the connections are inaccessible, the device is not online, or the panel still shows No Signal after the approved checks. Provide the device ID, selected input, visible message and what changed. Do not infer an OS fault or begin reinstallation from No Signal alone.
