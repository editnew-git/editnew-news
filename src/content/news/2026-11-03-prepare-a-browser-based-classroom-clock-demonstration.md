Use this checklist to rehearse an approved classroom clock demonstration on the equipment staff will see. The aim is to confirm that the clock, branding, and ordinary presentation controls work in that demonstration environment.

## Before you begin

Have the approved demonstration page and control link, the intended screen and browser device, network access, and a school or organization contact who can confirm the setup. Ask your administrator or support contact to confirm the browser and access requirements for your demonstration.

Use a designated test environment. An ordinary clock demonstration does not establish that a setup is ready for operational emergency use.

## Check the demonstration

1. **Confirm the page and account.** Open the page supplied for your demonstration. Check its name, clock, and branding with your contact before sharing it with staff.
2. **Use the actual display equipment.** Open the demonstration using the browser, screen, and network planned for the session. A screen connected to the internet may still need a separate device with a suitable browser. Record the equipment and browser used.
3. **Check what the audience can see.** View the clock and logo from the intended viewing distance. Check that the page fits the display and that text and branding are readable.
4. **Identify the operator and controls.** Confirm which link shows the clock and which link controls it. Treat a QR code or control link as access to the demonstration until its permissions are confirmed. Have one designated operator use the controls during the rehearsal.
5. **Rehearse ordinary functions.** Use the instructions supplied with the template to change the clock style. If a presentation is included, show an approved sample and use the template's confirmed method to return to the clock. Keep the display visible while testing.
6. **Record the result before sharing.** Note anything that did not load, respond, fit, or return to the expected view. Resolve those issues before inviting staff to use the controls.

## Expected result

The intended screen displays a readable clock and approved branding. The designated operator can demonstrate the agreed ordinary functions and return to the normal clock view. You have a record of the equipment and browser that were tested.

## If a check fails

Send your administrator or [EditNew Support](https://support.editnew.ca) the demonstration name, device and browser details, the time of the check, and what you expected compared with what appeared. Include a screenshot where useful, keeping private links and account information within the support conversation.
