Use this guide to configure a S.A.M. device for Mountain Standard Time (MST) year-round by changing its time-zone override to `America/Phoenix`.

## Before you begin

- Sign in to your S.A.M. account.
- Confirm which device you need to update.
- Make sure you have permission to edit and save the device configuration.

## Step 1: Open the Signs section

1. In the left navigation menu, select **Signs**.
2. Locate the device you want to update.
3. Select the device ID or wrench icon to open its configuration.

![S.A.M. Signs page highlighting the Signs menu and the selected device configuration controls](https://media.wwds.co/u/pressly-pr-en-20261104-001-2dd5310630371d6170f9633aa0bc1411c8a2e3dd96a10a976adb0aa7712f4ebc.webp)

## Step 2: Open General settings

1. On the device configuration page, select the **General** tab.
2. Locate the **General** settings panel.
3. Find the **Override Time Zone** menu.

![S.A.M. General settings page showing the Override Time Zone field](https://media.wwds.co/u/pressly-pr-en-20261104-001-b1ebaf054e22d499ee466a44991f5faad1ab19c42c5047c9cbf70b3a3a6e787c.webp)

## Step 3: Select the standard time zone

1. Open the **Override Time Zone** menu.
2. Scroll through the available time zones.
3. Select `America/Phoenix`.

![S.A.M. Override Time Zone dropdown with America Phoenix selected](https://media.wwds.co/u/pressly-pr-en-20261104-001-186ed84cae7452022d7f50b7dc9bd930faf09d99458aa50ebb6806b3015b83a5.webp)

> **Note:** `America/Phoenix` uses Mountain Standard Time (MST) year-round and does not observe Daylight Saving Time.

## Step 4: Save the changes

1. Select the green **Save** button at the top of the General settings page.
2. Wait for the settings to save.
3. Allow the device time to receive the updated configuration.

![S.A.M. General settings page highlighting the green Save button](https://media.wwds.co/u/pressly-pr-en-20261104-001-b657973b8a84e6a10d2541b734ddda0656666192daad03403701aa76d464d5e1.webp)

## Expected result

The device is configured to use Mountain Standard Time year-round and will not switch for Daylight Saving Time.
