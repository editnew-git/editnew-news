If your sign is offline, blank, or unresponsive after a power interruption, check its recovery before assuming the player needs repair. The interruption alone does not identify the cause.

## Before you begin

You need safe access to the player and the current restart instructions for that hardware. If power is still unstable, the installation is inaccessible, or there is an electrical concern, ask the responsible local team for help.

## Check recovery

1. **Identify the affected sign.** Note its location, sign or player ID if available, when power was interrupted, and what the screen shows now.

2. **Confirm power is available.** Have the responsible on-site person confirm that power has been restored. Check accessible player connections and note any indicator lights.

3. **Follow the approved restart instructions.** If the player has not recovered, use the power-cycle procedure supplied by support for that model. If you do not have those instructions, contact support before proceeding.

4. **Allow startup to finish.** Watch for normal playback or an error message. Follow the startup guidance supplied for the player.

5. **Verify both connection and playback.** Check whether the player returns online, or ask support to confirm. Then check the physical screen and confirm that the expected signage content is displaying.

## Expected result

The player is online and the screen displays the expected content. If it recovers, report the successful restart to support; recovery after a power cycle does not by itself establish the original cause.

## If it does not recover

Send support the player ID and location, the time of the interruption, the restart procedure followed, and a readable photo of any error. Mention whether the problem returned after initially recovering.

Support can then identify the appropriate next step for the hardware. Operating-system recovery and replacement require separate guidance.
