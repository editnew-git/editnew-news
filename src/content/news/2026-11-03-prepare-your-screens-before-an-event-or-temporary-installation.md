Prepare and test your S.A.M. screens before the event so the intended content is ready on the actual displays.

## Before you start

Have the agreed screen and equipment plan, access to the intended account, and an on-site contact. Confirm the setup with your equipment provider if any screen arrangement or connection is still uncertain.

## Prepare and test the setup

1. **Confirm each screen's purpose.** Record which screen will show which content, its orientation, and the dimensions required by the agreed installation. For a video wall or combined screens, confirm how the equipment will present the content before designing for it.

2. **Match the content to the display plan.** Keep portrait and landscape content organized for their intended screens. Check images and videos for stretching, cropping, or unwanted borders. Do not assume one set of dimensions fits every installation.

3. **Test before travelling or opening.** Use the intended players and screens where available. Check the content sequence and any presentation controls the on-site team will use. Ask support to help with account or device assignments that you cannot verify.

4. **Allow the players to receive the content.** Connect through the approved network while reliable internet access is available. Confirm that the intended content reaches each player. Agree a backup connection with the responsible team if venue connectivity may be limited; use a phone hotspot only where approved and configured for the equipment.

5. **Check the final installation.** Once the actual displays are connected, inspect each screen for the correct content, orientation, and fit. Ask the installer to resolve cabling or physical-access problems. Report any remaining content or display adjustments before the screens are needed.

6. **Confirm the on-site support plan.** Make sure the responsible contact knows which equipment belongs to each screen, how to reach support, and what approved backup equipment or connection is available. Leave time to test again after final changes.

## Expected result

Each installed screen shows its intended content, and the on-site team knows how to request help if something changes.

If the final display differs from your test, send support the screen or player name, location, a clear photo, and the difference you see. Use your agreed account route or check [EditNew Support](https://support.editnew.ca) for current contact details.
