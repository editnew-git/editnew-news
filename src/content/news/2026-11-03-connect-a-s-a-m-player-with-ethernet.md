Use Ethernet to connect a S.A.M. player when support has confirmed the wired connection supported by your hardware and site IT has approved the network access.

## Before you begin

Have the player, its approved power supply, display connection, Ethernet cable, and any adapter specifically confirmed for that player. Ask site IT to identify an approved network port that provides the required internet access.

If you are unsure which connector or adapter to use, confirm it with support before proceeding.

## Connect and verify

1. **Identify the player.** Record the sign or player ID if available and confirm where it will be installed.

2. **Connect the approved network cable.** Follow the hardware instructions to connect the player's supported Ethernet connection to the port identified by site IT. Check that the accessible connections are seated.

3. **Connect the display and power.** Follow the setup instructions for the player. Select the TV input connected to it and allow the player to start.

4. **Confirm the player is online.** Check its status through your authorized account access, or ask support to confirm. A cable connected to a wall port, or lights at the network port, does not by itself establish internet access.

5. **Verify the physical screen.** Confirm that the expected content is displaying. Have support confirm any required configuration or content synchronization before considering setup complete.

## Expected result

The player is online through the approved Ethernet connection and the physical screen shows the expected content.

## If the player remains offline

Record the sign or player ID, screen message, and which network port is in use. If safely visible, note whether the cable is connected and whether any port lights appear.

Ask site IT to verify that the approved connection provides the required access, and send your observations to support. Keep network-configuration changes with the responsible IT team.
