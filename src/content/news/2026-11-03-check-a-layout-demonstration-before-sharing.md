Before sharing an interactive layout demonstration, confirm what visitors can see and what its links or QR codes allow them to change. A demonstration should use approved example content and be separate from live screens.

## Before you begin

Have permission to share the content and a demonstration environment approved by the person responsible for the layout. Confirm the intended audience and whether visitors should only view the demonstration or interact with it.

## Review the demonstration

1. **Confirm that it is a separate example.** Ask the layout owner to verify that the demonstration is detached from live screens. A copied layout or a different name alone does not establish that its controls and content references are isolated.
2. **Review the content.** Remove private client details, personal information, credentials, internal instructions, and material that is not approved for that audience.
3. **Check every link and QR code.** Confirm its destination and the actions available to a recipient. A QR code can open active controls; it is not automatically a viewing link.
4. **Keep live controls out of the shared page.** Do not include production control links. If a demonstration link exposes media upload, editing, or other capabilities beyond the approved interaction, withhold it and ask the owner or support for an appropriate sharing method.
5. **Test the recipient experience.** Have the owner arrange an authorized check using the intended visitor access. Review the embedded view on desktop and mobile and confirm that it shows the correct example.
6. **Obtain the sharing review.** Confirm the content, destinations, permitted interactions, and person responsible for maintaining the demonstration before distributing it.

## Expected result

The shared page contains only approved demonstration material, and its reviewed links expose only the interactions approved for the intended audience. No live screen controls are included.

If the access scope cannot be verified, keep the interactive link or QR code unshared until it can be reviewed.
