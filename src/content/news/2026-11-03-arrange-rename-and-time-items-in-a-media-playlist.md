Identify playlist items by their content, then check saved labels, order and still-image duration separately.

## Open the intended Media module

Confirm the layout ID and attached displays. If the sidebar name is unclear, use **gear tab → Module Manager** to identify the Media type and ID, return with the **house tab**, and open that module.

## Labels and duration

Open the intended item's **wrench menu → Config/Schedule**. The inspected form exposes **Item Label** and **Duration (seconds)**. Enter a useful label and, for the applicable still image, the intended duration. Use that form's green **SAVE**, then reopen it to check the values.

Changing Item Label does not rewrite words embedded in an image. Duration also differs from eligibility: UTC start/end bounds, month days and the device-local weekly grid determine when an item may play. Preserve those settings unless the schedule itself is in scope.

## Playlist order

The inspected Media list says **Drag thumbnail to reorder**. Move the intended thumbnail within that list, use the Media editor's main **SAVE**, then reopen Media to check the stored order. If the visible form does not offer that behavior or the order fails to persist, stop and ask support; do not repeatedly rearrange unidentified items.

## Observe the sequence

Reload the layout's Preview and watch a complete eligible cycle. Compare order, readability and still-image display time. Inspect the relevant schedule when an expected item is absent. Video timing and other media types require their own checks.

Check the assigned physical display separately. A saved duration or correct browser sequence does not certify physical playback timing.
