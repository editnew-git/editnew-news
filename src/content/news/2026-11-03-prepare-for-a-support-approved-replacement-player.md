Use this checklist after support or your responsible reseller has arranged a replacement player for a screen that is not operating correctly.

## Before the replacement arrives

1. Confirm the replacement has been approved and record the support reference. Confirm the delivery address, recipient and installation contact through your existing support conversation.
2. Identify the old sign or player and the physical screen it serves. Record the intended layout and content. If the sign number is uncertain, ask support to identify it before the swap.
3. Tell support whether the installation uses a wired network connection or Wi-Fi. Confirm what network preparation is required and who will provide it. Share any required credentials only through the secure method agreed with support.
4. Mention any installation-specific settings or display adjustments that must carry over. Ask support to confirm the account, layout and preparation of this particular replacement.
5. Arrange a time when the onsite contact can follow the supplied installation instructions and observe the screen. Keep the old equipment and its accessories available until support confirms what to return.

## Check the replacement

Follow the installation instructions supplied for your replacement model. Once it is connected, check that the intended content appears on the correct physical screen and has the expected size and orientation. Confirm with support that the replacement is associated with the intended account and layout.

**Expected result:** the replacement shows the agreed content on the intended screen, and support has enough information to confirm the swap.

## If playback is still wrong

Record what the screen shows, the time of the check and any visible error. Send a clear photo and the old and replacement device identifiers through your existing support conversation. Pause further changes until support gives you the next step.

Replacement delivery and the old equipment's return can have separate instructions. Use [Prepare an equipment return for pickup](https://support.editnew.ca/news/2026-11-02-prepare-an-equipment-return-for-pickup/) once your return is authorized.
