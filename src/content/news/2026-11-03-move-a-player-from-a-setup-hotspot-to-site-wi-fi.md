Keep the setup hotspot available until your player has received its site Wi-Fi settings and required content. Saving a network profile does not by itself confirm that an offline player has received it or connected to that network.

## Before you begin

You need a supported player, an authorized site Wi-Fi network, and current setup instructions from support. Arrange for support or the authorized administrator to confirm the network configuration and the handoff.

## Move to site Wi-Fi

1. **Identify the player and destination network.** Confirm the sign or player ID and the site network it should use with support and the responsible site IT contact.

2. **Bring the player online through the setup hotspot.** Follow the instructions supplied for your player and phone. Have support confirm that the intended player is online through the temporary connection.

3. **Have the site profile configured.** The authorized administrator or support team should apply the approved Wi-Fi settings. A profile listed in the account is a configuration choice; it is not proof that the player is currently using that network.

4. **Keep the hotspot on while synchronization completes.** Let the player receive the required settings and content. Ask support to confirm readiness before disconnecting. Download time depends on what the player needs, so do not rely on a fixed number of minutes.

5. **Turn off the hotspot when directed.** Keep the player powered and allow it to connect to the site network. Have support verify the connection after the hotspot is off.

6. **Check the physical screen.** Confirm that the expected content is displaying and report any error or missing update.

## Expected result

The player remains online through the authorized site Wi-Fi after the hotspot is turned off, and the physical screen shows the expected content. Both checks matter: visible content alone does not confirm the network handoff.

## If it goes offline again

Tell support when the hotspot was turned off, which player was affected, and what the screen shows. The site connection has not yet been verified. Follow support's direction about restoring the temporary connection while the network settings or site access are checked.
