Use this checklist to help a new content owner work in the correct S.A.M. account and update the right screens.

## Before you start

Confirm organizational ownership and involve the authorized account administrator. Have the screen locations, sign or player names, active layout names, and the agreed support route available.

## Complete the handoff

1. **Confirm authorized access.** Ask the incoming user to sign in to [RunSam.com](https://runsam.com) using their authorized access. Confirm they can see the expected account and screens. If access is missing or incorrect, ask the account administrator or support to resolve it.

2. **Map screens to layouts.** Record each physical screen's location, its sign or player name, and the layout it currently uses. Include the content area the team normally edits. Have someone at the location help verify the mapping if the names are unclear.

3. **Agree who maintains the content.** Identify the person responsible for routine updates and the on-site contact who can check the physical display. Explain which screens share a layout so everyone understands where an edit may appear.

4. **Practise one approved update.** Choose an appropriate test time and a small, approved piece of content. Open the confirmed active layout, make the change in its intended content area, and save it. Review the preview and have the on-site contact confirm the result on the affected screen. Stop and check the mapping if the expected screen does not change.

5. **Share the learning path.** Start with signing in, checking screen status, opening the active layout, and uploading approved images. Cover scheduling and more advanced features when the new user needs them. Use [EditNew Support](https://support.editnew.ca) for support and training resources.

6. **Record the support arrangements.** Share the agreed account support route and the information needed to identify a screen when asking for help. Keep the handoff notes available to authorized team members without including passwords, recovery codes, or private remote-control links.

## Expected result

The incoming content owner can identify the correct account, screen, layout, and content area, complete an approved update, and reach the right contact for help.

Account ownership changes, device transfers, and access provisioning should go through your authorized administrator or support. If the account or screen mapping is uncertain, resolve that before making further content changes.
