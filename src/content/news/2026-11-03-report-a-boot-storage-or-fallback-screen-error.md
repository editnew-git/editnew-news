If a player stops during startup or shows a storage or fallback message instead of signage, capture the error before trying recovery steps. The screen alone may not identify the cause.

## Before you begin

You need a safe view of the player and display. Ask the responsible local team for help if the installation is inaccessible or there is an electrical concern.

## Record the problem

1. **Identify the player.** Record its sign or player ID, location, and model if known. Include any software version already visible on the screen.

2. **Photograph the message clearly.** Take a photo of the full screen and a closer view if the text is small. Check that the words are readable before sending it. Copy the exact error text if you can.

3. **Describe when it appears.** Note whether the error appears immediately at startup, during an update, or after the player had been running. Include any recent power interruption, update, or configuration change.

4. **Record what has already been tried.** If the player has already been restarted, say whether the same error returned. Include any temporary recovery and what happened next. Do not repeat a restart just to reproduce the error unless support directs you.

5. **Send the evidence to support.** Include the player details, photos, timing, and actions already taken. Ask support to confirm the hardware and the appropriate next step.

## Expected result

Support can identify the affected player and review the actual error. A player briefly returning online or downloading content does not establish that a recurring startup error has been repaired.

## Follow the recovery guidance

Wait for instructions specific to the device before entering commands, attempting installation again, or using recovery media. Preserve the error evidence so support can assess the problem.

After an authorized recovery action, confirm both normal startup and the expected content on the physical screen. Report any error that returns.
