If your S.A.M. player appears offline or its screen has stopped receiving updates, start with the checks below. A player may continue showing downloaded content while offline, so its connection status and the picture on the TV are separate things to check.

## Before you begin

You or an on-site contact must be able to see the player and screen safely. If the installation is inaccessible or there is an electrical concern, stop and ask the responsible local team for help.

## Check the player

1. **Identify what is affected.** Record the sign or player ID if available, its location, and when the problem began. Check whether other players at the site are also affected.

2. **Find the player and check power.** The TV and the player are separate devices. Check that the player's accessible power connection is in place, and note any indicator lights. If you cannot identify the player or its power control, ask support for guidance for that model.

3. **Check the TV input and screen.** Make sure the TV is using the input connected to the player. Report exactly what you see: a black screen, No Signal, another message, old or incorrect content, or normal playback. Include the exact message and a photo if it helps explain the problem.

4. **Note network and power changes.** Tell support about recent power interruptions or changes to the site's router or network. If the player uses Ethernet and the connection is safely visible, note whether the cable is connected and whether any lights appear at the port. Port lights alone do not confirm internet access.

5. **Follow a temporary-connection test if support directs you.** Use the current instructions supplied for your player and phone. Report whether the player comes online through the hotspot and what the screen shows. Keep the temporary connection available until support confirms that the required synchronization and connection checks are complete.

## Expected result

These checks should give support a clear picture of the affected player, its power and screen state, and its connection symptoms. If the player comes online, also check that the physical screen is showing the expected content.

A successful hotspot test helps investigate the normal network connection. A failed test does not prove that the player is damaged. Temporary connectivity also does not confirm that the site's usual connection has been restored.

## If the player is still offline

Send support:

- The sign or player ID, if available, and its location.
- When the problem began and whether other players are affected.
- What the screen shows, including any exact message or useful photo.
- Your power and network observations, including recent changes.
- The checks already tried and the result of any support-directed test.

Ask the responsible site IT or facilities team to help with network or electrical issues. For additional guides, visit [EditNew Support](https://support.editnew.ca).
