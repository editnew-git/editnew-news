Use these checks when a supplied link or QR code opens an unexpected Remote Control page, shows the wrong layout, or offers controls you were not expecting. Confirm the destination before using or sharing it.

## Before you begin

Have the link supplied by your account owner or support team and the name of the sign or layout you are authorized to control. Know the purpose of the link, such as operating a demonstration or using a particular support control.

## Check the link

1. **Confirm the intended target.** Ask the sender to identify the sign or layout and the controls the link should provide. A page's familiar logo or title alone is not enough to confirm its target.
2. **Open the supplied link without operating controls.** Note the page title, target details if shown, and the controls offered. If you scanned a QR code, record where that code was supplied or displayed.
3. **Compare the view with the instructions.** Check whether you received the expected display page or control page. Record any unexpected layout, upload option, or device control.
4. **Stop if the target or access is wrong.** Do not try controls to discover what they affect, and do not change numbers or other parts of the URL. Ask the account owner or support team for a confirmed replacement.
5. **Retest the corrected link in the intended context.** Use the device and user access intended for the recipient. Have the sender confirm the target and available controls. Perform a functional check only when it has been authorized and someone can observe the correct display.
6. **Use the confirmed version.** Replace your saved bookmark or personal copy of the code after confirmation. If the incorrect version was shared or printed, tell its owner where it was used so they can arrange the correction.

## Expected result

The confirmed link opens the intended view with the expected controls for the authorized recipient. Any agreed functional check affects the intended display.

## Ask for help

Send your account owner or [EditNew Support](https://support.editnew.ca) the intended target, where you received the link or code, what opened, and a screenshot of the unexpected view. Keep the full control link and QR code within that support conversation; they may provide access to controls.
