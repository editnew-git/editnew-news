An online player may still be receiving an update, and its connected screen may not be showing the expected content. Use these checks to separate the player's connection, content synchronization, and visible playback.

## Before you begin

Have access to the intended player and its layout. If you are checking an update, note what changed and when you saved it, including the time zone. Arrange an on-site observation or a current photo of the physical screen.

## Check each source of information

1. **Confirm the player and its connection.** Check the intended player's identity and assigned layout. Review its status and last check-in time, if shown. A recent check-in tells you that the player communicated with the service; it does not establish continuous connectivity or visible playback. Connection history, when available, can help show intermittent access.

2. **Check synchronization separately.** Saving an update and seeing the player online do not confirm that the player has received everything it needs. Review any available synchronization information and keep the connection available while the update transfers. Intermittent connectivity can delay new content. Content loaded from an external website also depends on that website being reachable.

3. **Review the correct layout in Preview.** Look for the intended content in the layout assigned to the player. Preview helps you check the layout, but it does not prove that the player has received the latest version or that the physical screen is displaying it.

4. **Check screenshot freshness, when available.** If your account provides player screenshots, or support supplies one, compare the capture time with the update time. An older screenshot cannot verify a later change. Allow for capture and delivery to take time; use the timestamp rather than assuming a fixed refresh interval. A player screenshot shows the player's rendered output, so physical-screen confirmation is still needed.

5. **Observe the physical screen.** Confirm that the intended screen shows the expected content. If a TV or display indicator disagrees with what you see, record both observations. A status symbol alone is not enough to identify a cable, display, network, or player fault.

## Expected result

For a completed update, the intended content is visible on the correct physical screen. You can also identify the layout checked, the available connection and synchronization information, and the age of any screenshot used.

If nobody can observe the screen, record that visible playback remains unverified.

## When the results disagree

Contact [EditNew Support](https://support.editnew.ca) with the player and layout identifiers, the change and save time, available status or synchronization information, screenshot capture time, and the on-site result. A current photo and the exact screen message help explain the mismatch.
