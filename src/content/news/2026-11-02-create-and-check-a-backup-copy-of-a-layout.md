Create a separate layout copy and check it before making a change that may be difficult to reverse.

## Copy the intended layout

1. Open the source layout and confirm its ID. Finish saving or deliberately cancel any pending edits first.
2. Select the **gear tab at the top of the right sidebar**.
3. Select **Copy — Duplicate current layout**.
4. In **COPY LAYOUT**, confirm the source shown under **COPY SELECTED LAYOUT**. Enter a distinct name in **Label**.
5. Select the dialog's green **Save**.
6. Return to **Layouts** and find the copy by its new label. Record its **different layout ID**, then open that copy.
7. Inspect the modules and content you need to preserve. Open the copy's own **Preview** and check its appearance.

The copy workflow creates another layout. Keep the source and copy IDs separate, especially when their names are similar.

## Check before relying on the copy

Compare important media, text, order, durations, schedules and source connections. Check **Attached Devices** before editing or assigning the copy. A correct Preview does not prove every property or device assignment was duplicated.

A copy is not an undo operation on the original layout. This procedure does not restore content into the original ID or guarantee recovery of sign assignments. Do not deploy a backup simply because its Preview looks correct.
