A sign is the player connected to a physical screen. A layout contains the content that player is assigned to show. Use this checklist to choose the intended layout and understand which screens a change could affect.

## Before you begin

Have permission to manage the intended sign, its current layout, and the layout you want to use. Know which physical screen the player serves, and arrange a way to check that screen after the change.

## Plan and verify the change

1. **Identify the intended screen and player.** Match the physical location to the correct sign in your account. Record its identifier and current layout so you can describe the existing setup accurately.
2. **Open the proposed layout.** Review its content in Preview. Check that the content, orientation, and any scheduled items suit the intended screen.
3. **Check which other signs use that layout.** Several signs can share one layout. Editing shared content can affect every sign assigned to it, so confirm that the proposed content is suitable for all of those screens.
4. **Keep preparation separate from the live assignment.** A new layout can be prepared and reviewed before a sign is assigned to it. Confirm that your working layout has no live signs attached before treating it as a test.
5. **Confirm the assignment change with the person managing the sign.** Specify the exact player and destination layout. If you manage it yourself, use the assignment process confirmed for your account; contact support if the controls or target are unclear. Check the resulting assignment before making further changes.
6. **Allow synchronization and observe the screen.** Keep the player's connection available and check the actual display. A layout visible in Preview or an assignment shown in the account does not alone confirm that the screen has received the change.

## Expected result

The intended player is assigned to the chosen layout and the physical screen shows the expected content. Other signs retain their intended assignments.

If the player connects only intermittently, plan the change around an opportunity to synchronize and verify it before making another change.

## When to contact support

Contact [EditNew Support](https://support.editnew.ca) if you cannot identify the correct player, the layout assignment is unclear, or the screen continues to show different content. Include the player identifier, current and intended layout identifiers, change time, and what is visible on-site.

For the final check, follow [Verify a saved update on the physical display](https://support.editnew.ca/news/2026-11-02-verify-a-saved-update-on-the-physical-display/).
