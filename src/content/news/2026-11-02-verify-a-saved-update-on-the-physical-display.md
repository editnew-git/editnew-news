Check the saved layout, its Preview and the physical panel separately before calling an update complete.

## When this applies

Use this checklist after an authorized content change to a layout assigned to a display. It does not change assignments, force synchronization or restart a device.

## Before you begin

Have the layout ID, device ID, intended change and Save time with timezone. Arrange an observation of the actual panel when possible.

## Check each stage

1. Reopen the changed item and verify that the intended content was saved.
2. Reload Preview from the correct layout and wait for the changed item to appear.
3. Confirm the physical device is associated with the intended layout. A backup copy has a separate ID.
4. Record the device's actual status and synchronization information. Do not infer connectivity from a user-editable device name.
5. If using a stored screenshot, check its capture time. An old screenshot cannot establish delivery of a later edit.
6. Observe the physical panel showing the changed content. A player screenshot alone does not prove the panel is powered and displaying it.

## Expected result

The saved editor, fresh Preview and physical panel agree on the changed content. Record which stages are still unverified if an observation is unavailable.

## When to contact support

If Preview is correct but the panel is wrong, provide the layout and device IDs, Save time, device status, screenshot age and on-site result. Do not promise a fixed synchronization delay or repeatedly Republish as a cure for an offline device.
