Use this checklist when presentation content appears over an expected clock or alert, or when the clock flashes through a presentation. A clear record of the affected view helps support investigate the layout without changing a live setup by trial and error.

## Before you begin

Identify the layout or template, the affected sign or demonstration, and the person authorized to test it. Use an approved test environment for any reproduction. During a live emergency, follow your organization's emergency procedure.

## Record the behavior

1. **Identify where it happens.** Note whether you are looking at a layout preview, a demonstration page, or a physical screen. Record the layout name, target, and time.
2. **Capture the starting view.** Take a screenshot or photo of the unexpected content before changing anything. State what should have been visible.
3. **Record the control sequence.** Write down the presentation item and control used immediately before the problem. Distinguish content that appeared on its own from content selected by an operator.
4. **Describe the pattern.** Note whether the content stays over the clock or alert, appears only during a transition, or returns after a delay. A short recording can help show a brief flash.
5. **Compare only within the approved test.** If support asks you to reproduce the issue, record the ordinary playback first, then the agreed presentation sequence. If the item's schedule or duration is visible, record its current values without changing them.
6. **Record the result of clearing.** If your approved procedure includes a confirmed presentation-clear action, note the exact control used and what remains afterward. If the control is unclear or the expected view does not return, stop the test and report that result.

## Expected result

Your report shows which view was affected, how the content appeared, whether the problem persists or occurs during transitions, and what happened after the confirmed clear action. Support can use this evidence to distinguish playback settings, layout behavior, and a stale viewing page.

## Send the report

Contact your administrator or [EditNew Support](https://support.editnew.ca) with the layout and target details, the time, screenshots or recording, and the control sequence. Include whether another authorized view showed the same result.

Do not delete presentation items or change layering, scheduling, durations, or effects as an improvised fix. Keep any repair and follow-up test within the support instructions for that layout.
